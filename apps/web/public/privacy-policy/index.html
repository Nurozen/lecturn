Privacy notice

Lecturn is operated by Cloud Gatherer Labs. It is a fork of the open-source T3 Code project. This notice describes the Lecturn deployment at lecturn.cloudgatherer.net and relay.cloudgatherer.net.

Your account and connections

Clerk handles account sign-in and authentication. Our hosted services store account and linked-environment identifiers and connection metadata needed to connect your clients to your computer. Cloudflare provides hosting and connection routing; PlanetScale stores service data. Resend delivers service emails.

Your coding work

Your computer runs the coding agents and stores local project and conversation state. A remote client exchanges application traffic through the connection service. Your chosen model and coding-agent providers process the prompts, code, and other information you send to them under their own policies. Do not assume remote traffic or diagnostic records contain no sensitive information.

Analytics and diagnostics

Lecturn uses PostHog for product analytics and Axiom for operational diagnostics. Product analytics can be disabled in the app's privacy settings. That setting does not disable all local diagnostic logs or hosted operational logging needed to run and troubleshoot the service. Diagnostic data may include errors, timings, identifiers, and connection details. Automatic session replay and automatic interaction capture are not enabled for this deployment.

Questions and requests

For questions about your account, data access, or deletion, contact accounts@cloudgatherer.net. We will review requests for data controlled by this deployment; your independent provider accounts are managed by those providers.

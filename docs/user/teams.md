# Teams

Teams lets a company purchase Lecturn Connect seats centrally and manage who receives company access. Each person keeps their own Lecturn account, environments, projects, and provider credentials.

## Set up a company

Open account settings in the hosted Lecturn web app, then select **Teams**. Desktop and locally hosted clients offer **Manage Teams in browser** to open that page. Sign in with the same Lecturn account. Create a company, choose a seat quantity and billing interval, and complete checkout. The initial purchase supports 5–20 seats. Pricing is $15 per seat monthly or $150 per seat annually, before applicable tax.

Returning from checkout does not itself activate access. Use **Refresh team status** while payment confirmation arrives. Invite teammates by email, then assign seats explicitly after they join. An invitation or company membership alone does not consume a paid seat or grant Connect access. The owner also needs an assigned seat to use company Connect.

## Roles and seats

The owner manages billing and administrator roles. Owners and administrators can invite members, assign or revoke seats, review the company environment inventory, and set company policies. Members can see their own company access without seeing administrative inventory or billing details.

Increasing the purchased quantity shows a price preview before confirmation. Reductions take effect at renewal and cannot leave fewer seats than are assigned. To cancel a scheduled reduction, select the current purchased quantity and choose **Keep current seats**. Revoking an assigned seat frees it for another member; it does not reduce the purchased quantity.

## Choose who funds a connection

Select **Personal** or a company when setting up a new Connect environment. Company access requires an assigned seat and an active company subscription. Each seat supports up to three company-funded managed environments.

The selection funds new links only. An existing environment keeps its original funding until it is explicitly unlinked and linked again. Selecting a company does not transfer personal environments or cancel a personal subscription.

Company membership never grants access to another member’s computer. Administrators can see environment names, owners, and link status, but cannot browse projects, read conversations, use provider credentials, or connect to a member’s environment through Teams.

## Company policies

Administrators can choose allowed providers and whether company-funded environments may publish agent activity. **All providers** includes providers added in future releases; clearing every provider blocks provider work on company-funded hosts. Activity publishing controls notifications and Live Activities, separately from the managed connection itself.

Company-funded hosts enforce these settings. If company authorization cannot be checked, new provider operations and activity publishing stop until it can be verified again. Removing a member or revoking a seat ends company Connect access. Personal environments and local data remain yours.

The mobile app lets you choose company access and connect to your own environments. Use the hosted Lecturn web app for purchasing and team administration.

Shared worker pools, organization SSO provisioning, and device-management policies are not part of this Teams release.

## Closing an owner account

Deleting the company owner’s Lecturn account ends that company’s Connect access and cancels its subscription. Other members keep their personal accounts and personal Connect access. Ownership transfer is not available in this first release; contact support before deleting an owner account if the company needs to continue.
